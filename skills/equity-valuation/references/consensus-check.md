# Consensus check reference

Consensus is an expectation snapshot, not a verified future outcome. Load within `equity-valuation`; best-effort coverage can be missing or incomplete.

1. Use `finance_security_reference`, `finance_estimates` and `finance_fundamentals` when exposed. Preserve sources, cutoff/PIT metadata and original status; an unavailable field does not mean zero coverage. Continue supported fundamentals analysis without inventing consensus.
2. Separate reported annual/quarterly/TTM data from `periodType: estimate`. Forward EPS/revenue, recommendation averages and analyst targets are not reported company results. Analyst counts do not establish accuracy.
3. Test expectations against supported growth, margins, cash conversion and financial capacity. Disagreement is a question for investigation, not proof of value. Never claim estimate revisions, management guidance or earnings surprises without dated source evidence.
4. In valuation-first mode compare independent locked valuation, consensus and observed price only in a separate post-lock section with aligned listing/currency/time. Do not use consensus targets to calibrate the independent model. In intrinsic-only mode omit targets, ratings, price comparisons and upside; non-price estimates remain distinctly labelled external assumptions.

Output available and missing fields, historical/forward boundary, dated evidence, supporting/conflicting fundamentals and a confidence/coverage caveat. Preserve conflicts and uncertainty in the ledger when available; otherwise mark not persisted.
